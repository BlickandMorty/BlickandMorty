# Sensemaking, Radical Honesty & AI Systems Engineering

> *"In an age of abundant AI-generated proofs, human understanding may become more valuable than proof generation itself. [...] If AI can generate thousands of correct proofs, mathematicians can't possibly read, understand, verify, and teach all of them. So the scarce resource in mathematics shifts from finding proofs to making sense of them."*  
> — **Terence Tao**, *Mathematics in the Age of AI* ([arXiv:2608.16753](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.16753), August 2026)

### 🔬 The "Learning in Public" Manifesto: AI Scaffolding vs. Human Understanding

I am an undergraduate student studying **Chemical Physics at Columbia University** with a background in AI red-teaming (Mercor) and tactical operations (U.S. Army National Guard).

In an era where frontier AI models can effortlessly synthesize code and formal proof syntax, **raw generation is abundant and cheap—genuine human comprehension, sensemaking, and rigorous verification are what matter.**

This GitHub profile is deliberately built around **radical epistemic transparency**:

1. **AI as an Exploratory Scaffold:** I use modern AI systems as cognitive force multipliers to map out large formal structures, Lean 4 theorem declarations, and exploratory mathematics (such as EML operators and lattice abstractions).
2. **Honest Demarcation of Mastery:** While AI helped generate a large corpus of 246 theorem candidates across Epistemos and EML, **I currently only have true, independent mathematical mastery over the first 7 foundational theorems and core identities**. The remainder of the advanced theoretical formalisms represents the outer boundary of my current knowledge—serving as my open, multi-year learning roadmap at Columbia.
3. **AI Evaluation & Failure Logging:** By comparing what models generate against what I can formally verify and understand, I use these repositories as active testbeds for **red-teaming AI reasoning**—logging where LLMs generate valid proof terms versus where they produce subtle mathematical hallucinations, invalid branch assumptions, or empty tautologies.

## How I Work

My recurring engineering loop is:
`frame -> inspect -> propose -> test -> falsify -> repair -> verify -> stop`

I separate observation from inference, attach tests or evidence to completion claims, preserve uncertainty, and prefer bounded failure over confident invention. In research repositories, “formal,” “analytic,” “conditional,” “numerical,” and “open” are deliberately different statuses.
